# Rare Books of the Lausanne University Library Collections (15th-16th centuries)

## Catalogue of the incunables preserved at the Lausanne University Library (2020)
ABRAHAM Caleb, Catalogue des incunables conservés à la Bibliothèque Cantonale Universitaire de Lausanne, 2020, 209 pp. Open Access: [academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/50838953/Catalogue_des_incunables_conserv%C3%A9s_%C3%A0_la_Biblioth%C3%A8que_Cantonale_Universitaire_de_Lausanne?source=swp_share)
This project was conducted as part of an internship done at the Lausanne University Library Rare Books Department in 2020 (February-May).
All 208 incunables preserved at the Rare Books Department were identified and described according to bibliographical standards. 
The catalogue is sorted in alphabetical order according to the names of the authors.
